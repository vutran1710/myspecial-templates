# Rice paper

The house paper of MySpecial: gift tags hung from a red thread, a taped note for your message, a ribbon and a bow,
all on wrapping paper with blossoms. It is the Paper kit's look, and the default for new pages.

- Ground: `--ms-ground: wrap` (the page engine draws the wrapping paper behind every screen; the theme paints none).
- Fonts: Newsreader 400 roman and italic (variable optical size, weight fixed at 400), Be Vietnam Pro 400/500/600.
  SIL OFL, subset to Latin + Vietnamese (`licenses/`).
- Texture: the laid sheet of the note and the tags (WebP).
- Wide screens: the note on the left, the gifts in a row on the right.
